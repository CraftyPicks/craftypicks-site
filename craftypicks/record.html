What this record can actually prove

Two ways to be judged, and one of them is far faster

A win-loss record is the slowest possible evidence of a betting edge. Proving a real 5% edge from profit alone takes on the order of a few thousand bets. Proving the same thing by consistently beating the number the market closes at takes closer to fifty, because it measures the price rather than the outcome — and the price is what we actually claim to be good at.
