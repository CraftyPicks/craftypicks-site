Anytime touchdown

Each player's chance of scoring at least once, from how often he scores and how often the defence he faces allows it.

There is no book line beside it. This board costs nothing to produce — it reads a public file of weekly stats and the published schedule. Passing touchdowns do not count toward a quarterback's own chance here; a quarterback who throws three has not scored one.

How it has done

Every projection here is graded the next morning, whether or not anyone bet it. This is not a record; it is a calibration. A group called 25% should score about 25% of the time.
