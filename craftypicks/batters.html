The chance combines three rates — the batter's home runs per plate appearance, the pitcher's home runs per batter faced, and the league average — by the odds ratio, then multiplies by the park and by how often this batter comes to the plate. A starter is modelled as facing 58% of a lineup's plate appearances and a league-average bullpen the rest. Park factors are computed from home and away splits on both sides of the ball and then halved toward neutral, because one season of them is noisy. These are not lineups. Lineups post about two hours before first pitch and this board is built in the morning, so a rested regular will still appear.
