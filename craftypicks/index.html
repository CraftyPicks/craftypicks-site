How it works

Three steps. No group chat, no upsell.

Most touts sell you the pick and hide the record. We do the opposite: the card is free and the record is the product.

01

The whole board gets priced

Every game is pulled from 6+ sportsbooks each morning. Both sides of each market get the vig stripped out, which turns a book's price into its honest opinion of the odds.

02

Only real outliers get posted

Average those honest opinions and you have a consensus fair price. A play posts only when the best number on the board beats that consensus by 2.5% or more. Cap is 6 plays a day.

03

Everything gets graded

The next morning every play is marked win, loss, or push against the final score and added to the permanent log. Nothing is deleted, re-priced, or quietly disappeared.
